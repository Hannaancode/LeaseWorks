The original JPEG files were downloaded on 5 October 2026 without cropping, retouching or recompression. No pixels were generated. The cited licences apply to the photographs separately from the application code.

The wall-mounted AC has visible surface marks. The coil photograph shows corrosion and bent fins. The heater photograph shows a storage tank and fittings. The faucet photograph shows flowing water, which does not prove a leak. None establishes age, hidden faults, operational performance, repair cost or a cause of damage.

In offline mode I provide explicitly labelled reference assessments only for the exact hashes of these files. They are fixed test responses, not AI vision. Live mode sends the actual image bytes to the configured vision model; arbitrary uploads require that mode for model inference.
